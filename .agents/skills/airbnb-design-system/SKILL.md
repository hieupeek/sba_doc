# Airbnb Design System (Hospitality & Lodging)

> Category: Hospitality Design System
> Use for: Hotel guest interfaces, room catalog cards, stay reservations, check-in forms, and review components.

## 1. Visual Theme & Hospitality Principles
- **Cereal Typography**: Clear, inviting geometric sans-serif hierarchy.
- **Rausch Red / Coral**: `#FF385C` primary brand accent for bookings.
- **Babu Teal & Arches Peach**: Supporting accents for amenities and tags.
- **Card-driven containment**: Soft rounded borders (`border-radius: 12px`), clean photo aspect ratios, and instant amenity badges.

Refer to [`tokens.css`](./tokens.css), [`DESIGN.md`](./DESIGN.md), and [`components.html`](./components.html) in this directory for full specifications.
